Last Pixel Standing for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

About the game

Last Pixel Standing is a one-touch survival game. Every move destroys the floor behind you. There is a classic mode and a daily challenge. Optional purchases remove ads or unlock cosmetic trails. Trails do not change your score.

Contact

Email rafae.saleem.development@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and app version (iOS Settings → Last Pixel Standing) if something is broken.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore Remove Ads or a trail pack, open the Shop in the game and tap Restore Purchases. You need a network connection, and you should be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Refunds are requested from Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. The game can be played while signed out; scores still save on the device. Revived runs are not submitted to ranked boards. Game Center sign-in and account problems are handled by Apple.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes on-device scores and settings, Game Center, StoreKit purchases, AdMob ads, and Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Read the privacy policy.
