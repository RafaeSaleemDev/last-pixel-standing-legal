Privacy Policy

Last Pixel Standing · Last updated 30 September 2026

Support

Who this covers

This policy describes how the iPhone and iPad game Last Pixel Standing handles information. The developer does not run a player account system. Contact: rafae.saleem.development@gmail.com.

Some features are provided by Apple and Google. Those companies process data under their own policies, linked below.

Stored on your device

The game saves the following in local storage on your device (UserDefaults). It stays on the device and is not uploaded to a developer server:

  • All-time best score and daily best score
  • Completed-run count, used to decide when a revive or an interstitial ad can be offered
  • Settings: sound effects on or off, volume, and haptics on or off
  • Which cosmetic trail is equipped, and which trails the device already knows are unlocked
  • Whether the tutorial has been completed

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you open leaderboards or a score is submitted, Apple Game Center receives that score. Apple uses your Game Center identity, which can include your Apple ID and display name. Play works without Game Center. Revived runs are not submitted to ranked leaderboards. Apple’s policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the trail pack are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your card number or billing address. Apple keeps the purchase record so Restore Purchases can return items you already bought. Trails are cosmetic and do not change scores.

Advertising

The game shows Google AdMob ads: a rewarded ad for an optional revive, and an interstitial when you leave results for Home after enough completed runs. Ads are not shown during active play. If you own Remove Ads, both formats are turned off.

After the tutorial, the game may ask for permission to track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, AdMob can use Apple’s advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and to measure ads. If you deny it, you can still play, and ads can still appear, without that identifier. Where required (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Google’s consent message is shown before ads are requested. You can refuse that consent and keep playing.

Google’s policies: Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports

When Firebase is configured for a release build, the game sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics. The events are limited to: app open, tutorial completed, run started, run ended, revive offered / accepted / completed, shop viewed, purchase started, purchase completed, leaderboard opened, share tapped, and ad shown.

Those events can include:

  • App version
  • Game mode (classic or daily)
  • Score, survival time in seconds, and a short death-reason code
  • Whether the run used a revive, and whether that revive was free or rewarded
  • Ad format (rewarded or interstitial)

The game does not send your name, email, Game Center display name, advertising identifier, touch path, or payment details in these events. Firebase may still attach technical data it collects by default, such as an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and a coarse location derived from IP address. If Crashlytics is enabled, crash reports can include the stack trace, device model, OS version, and app version.

If Firebase is not configured, these events are not sent. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Share

Share uses the system share sheet. The game does not upload the shared text. The destination you pick (Messages, Mail, and so on) handles it under that service’s own policy.

Children

Last Pixel Standing is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

How long data is kept, and deletion

  • On-device scores and settings remain until you delete the app or change them.
  • Game Center scores and purchase history are kept by Apple. Manage or delete your Apple data through Apple’s account tools.
  • Ad and analytics data is kept by Google according to Google’s retention rules. You can reset or limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, and you can refuse tracking when the app asks.

There is no developer account to delete. Email rafae.saleem.development@gmail.com if you have a question about data this game sent to Apple or Google, and the developer will explain which service holds it and how to reach that company.

Your choices

  • Deny tracking when iOS asks. Gameplay continues.
  • Turn off sound effects or haptics in Settings inside the game.
  • Skip Game Center. Local scores still save.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop rewarded and interstitial ads.
  • Limit ad tracking or reset the advertising identifier in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
